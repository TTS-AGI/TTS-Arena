title: Integrity policy
description: What model providers agree to when they submit, and what happens when it's broken.
---

TTS Arena only works if the votes are real. This page is the agreement every
provider accepts by submitting a model. It is short on purpose.

## The agreement

By submitting a model you agree that:

1. **Nobody on your side votes on it.** Not employees, contractors, interns,
   agencies, or anyone you ask - and not from personal accounts or "test
   accounts" either. If you want to hear your model next to others, type a
   custom prompt: those votes never touch the leaderboard.
2. **You don't try to find your model in a battle.** No matching your endpoint's
   request logs against arena sessions, no watermarks or tells in the audio, no
   other side channel that reveals which side is yours. The arena is blind by
   design, and anything that un-blinds it for you breaks the test.
3. **No bought, botted, or coordinated votes.** No paying or rewarding people
   for votes, no automation, no "everyone go vote today" to a community.
4. **You use the arena for ranking, not as an internal eval harness.** Sessions
   that are abandoned whenever your model isn't in them skew the data before
   anyone votes. Run internal comparisons with your own tooling, or with custom
   prompts here.
5. **You tell us who you are.** Anonymous entries included - see below.
6. **You answer when we ask.** If something looks wrong we contact the provider
   first. Answering honestly is part of the agreement.

## Anonymous entries

Codenamed entries are anonymous to voters, not to us. To run under a codename
you tell the TTS Arena team who you are, and we keep that confidential until
the reveal.

That confidentiality is a courtesy, not a contract. If the policy above is
broken, we may disclose the provider as part of the outcome.

## What happens when it's broken

- **The votes are excluded** and the leaderboard is recomputed from the clean
  set, so the model keeps nothing it gained.
- **The model is suspended.** It stays on the leaderboard, at the bottom, with a
  Suspended label saying why and when. The label has no expiry, and a suspended
  model can't be retracted or withdrawn.
- **We may publish what happened**: the model, the provider, what the data
  showed, and what the provider told us, including written correspondence with
  the arena. By submitting, you consent to that publication.
- **Relisting** the model, or listing a later model from the same provider, is
  at our discretion.

Nothing is deleted. Votes, sessions, and correspondence are kept so the record
can be checked.

Decisions under this policy are made by the TTS Arena team and are final.

## Why we're strict

A leaderboard that can be gamed is worse than none, because people trust it.
Every provider on the board is competing against the ones who follow the rules.
The policy exists to protect them.
