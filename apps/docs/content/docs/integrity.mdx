TTS Arena only works if the votes are real. Every provider accepts this policy
by submitting a model. The full terms are in the
[Provider Agreement](/provider-agreement); this page is the part that matters
most, kept short so there is no excuse for not reading it.

## Rules

1. **Nobody on your side votes on your model.** Not staff, contractors, agencies,
   or anyone you ask. Not from personal accounts, not from "test accounts".
2. **You don't un-blind the arena.** No matching endpoint logs to arena
   sessions, no audio tells, no side channel that reveals which side is yours.
3. **No bought, botted, or coordinated votes.**
4. **The arena is for ranking, not internal evaluation.** Want to hear your
   model next to others? Type a custom prompt - those votes never move the board.
5. **We know who you are**, even for codenamed entries, and you answer honestly
   when we ask.

## If it's broken

- The votes are excluded and the board is recomputed.
- The model is **suspended**: it stays on the leaderboard, at the bottom, with a
  label saying why and when. The label doesn't expire. Suspended models can't be
  retracted, renamed, or withdrawn.
- We may publish what happened, including what the provider told us.

Nothing is deleted, so the record can be checked. Decisions are made by the
TTS Arena team and are final.
