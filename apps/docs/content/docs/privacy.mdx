TTS Arena is a volunteer-run, open-source research project. We collect what we
need to keep the votes honest and to build a useful benchmark, and not much
else. This page says what that is in plain terms.

## What we collect

**When you sign in** with Hugging Face, we receive your Hugging Face user ID,
username, avatar, account creation date, and the email address on the account.
The account age is used for the 30-day voting requirement. The email is stored
so we can contact you about your account (for example, if it's been
quarantined) and is not used for anything else.

**When you use the arena**, we record:

- the prompts you submit and the audio each model produced for them;
- your votes, and how long you listened before voting;
- your IP address, browser user-agent, and a device fingerprint, on sign-in and
  with each vote;
- session events - battles started, whether they were voted on or abandoned,
  generation timings and errors;
- technical characteristics of your browser and device - things like screen and
  timezone settings, language, rendering and audio characteristics, and similar
  signals - collected by a small script while you use the arena. We store these
  as they're reported so we can tell real, varied listeners apart from many
  votes coming from one place dressed up to look like many.

A **captcha** may run once per browser. It stores a short-lived token and
nothing about you.

## Why

Almost all of this exists for one reason: a blind preference benchmark is only
worth something if the votes are real. IP, fingerprint, timing, and session
data are what let us spot automated voting, coordinated rings, and accounts
that can somehow recognise a model they shouldn't be able to. The
[voting](/voting) page describes how that system works.

The prompts and audio are the benchmark itself. They're what we and others can
use to study how people judge synthetic speech.

## What we publish

- The leaderboard: per-model ratings, uncertainty, win rates, and vote counts.
  Never per-voter data.
- Aggregate statistics and, from time to time, anonymised datasets of prompts,
  audio, and preferences. These contain no account identifiers, IP addresses,
  fingerprints, or email addresses.
- Accounts of integrity findings against model providers. These name the model
  and the provider, and can quote what the provider told us. They can describe
  the behaviour of accounts involved. See the
  [Provider Agreement](/provider-agreement).

## What we don't do

- We don't sell data, run ads, or share personal data with model providers.
  Providers see their model's ratings, not who voted.
- We don't send your identity to the model endpoints. They receive the prompt
  text, nothing else.
- We don't track you across other sites.

## Retention

We keep vote, session, and anti-abuse records indefinitely, because a decision
about the leaderboard has to remain checkable after the fact. Cached audio is
kept as long as it's useful to the benchmark.

If you want your account data removed, contact us. We'll delete your profile
information (username, email, avatar). Vote records may be retained in
anonymised form so the leaderboard's history remains consistent.

## Where it lives

The app runs on Hugging Face Spaces; the database runs on a server we operate.
Hugging Face handles the sign-in and has its own
[privacy policy](https://huggingface.co/privacy). TTS Arena is not affiliated
with Hugging Face.
